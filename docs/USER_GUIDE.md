# GMI MindSpace – Student user guide

*Your Space. Your Pace. Your Well-being.*

GMI MindSpace helps you check in with yourself, reflect, look after your well-being and reach
the GMI Counselling Unit. It is **not** an emergency service.

> **In danger or thinking about harming yourself?** Call **999** (or **112** from a mobile) or go
> to the nearest hospital emergency department now. Every screen has a **Get help** button.
> GMI MindSpace is not monitored 24/7 and nobody reads your entries in real time.

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Install** the app (your counsellor or the project team will send it; see
   [INSTALLATION.md](INSTALLATION.md)).
2. Open it and tap **Create an account**.
3. Enter your full name, student ID, student email and a password (at least 8 characters with a
   letter and a number). Read the privacy information and tick the box.
4. Open the confirmation email on your phone and tap the link, then **sign in**.
5. Allow notifications if you want reminders for appointments and activities.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password?** and follow the email.

## 2. Finding your way

The bar at the bottom has five tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your greeting, a quick mood check-in, shortcuts, your next appointment, upcoming activities |
| **Mood** | Check in and see your mood history and charts |
| **Self-care** | Breathing, grounding and gratitude exercises |
| **Booking** | Request and manage counselling appointments |
| **Profile** | Your details, notification settings, privacy, sign out |

Tapping the tab you are already on takes you back to its first screen.

## 3. The six modules

### Mood Check-in
- Pick how you feel (very happy, good, neutral, sad, stressed), optionally a reason and a short
  private note, then save.
- **History** and **weekly / monthly charts** show patterns over time.
- **Optional sharing:** you can let one counsellor see *how many* check-ins of each mood you had
  in a period. They never see your notes or reasons. You can stop sharing at any time, and
  sharing ends automatically after 30 days.

### My Journal
- Home ▸ **My Journal** ▸ **＋** to write. Use a prompt or write freely; mark gratitude entries.
- Search, edit and delete your entries.
- **Only you can read your journal.** Counsellors and administrators cannot read it.

### Self-care Corner
- **Breathe:** a guided breathing animation. Start and stop whenever you like.
- **Ground:** the 5-4-3-2-1 senses exercise.
- **Gratitude:** note three good things.
- Explore activities by category. These support everyday well-being; they are not a diagnosis
  or a replacement for professional care.

### Counselling Appointment
- **Booking** ▸ **Request appointment**: choose the kind of support, a date, a free time slot,
  and (optionally) a short note that only your counsellor sees.
- Your request is **pending** until the counsellor confirms it or suggests another time.
- You can cancel or ask to reschedule from the appointment details.
- You get a reminder 24 hours before (and 1 hour before on your phone) if reminders are on.

### Mental Health Learning Hub
- Home ▸ **Learning Hub**: articles, infographics, videos and FAQs by topic (stress, anxiety,
  sleep, relationships, academic burnout and more).
- Search, **bookmark** resources, and find them again under *Recently viewed*.

### Well-being Activities
- Home ▸ **Well-being Activities**: workshops and programmes on a calendar.
- Register (and cancel) from an activity's page. Online links appear after you register.
- After an activity starts you can leave feedback, anonymously if you prefer.

## 4. Your privacy

- Mood check-ins and journal entries are visible **only to you**.
- Counsellors see your name only when you book with them or join their activity, plus mood
  **counts** if you choose to share them.
- Notifications use generic wording (for example *"Your appointment request has been
  confirmed."*), never your details.
- Profile ▸ **Your privacy** explains what is stored and who can see it.

## 5. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| *"You seem to be offline"* | Check Wi-Fi / mobile data, then tap **Try again**. |
| *"Incorrect email or password"* | Check for typos; use **Forgot password?** if needed. |
| *"Please confirm your email first"* | Open the confirmation email on your phone and tap the link. |
| *"Your session has ended"* | Sign in again. This happens after a long time away. |
| No reminders | Profile ▸ Notification settings; also allow notifications for GMI MindSpace in phone Settings. |
| App will not install | See [INSTALLATION.md](INSTALLATION.md#installing-the-android-apk). |

Still stuck? Contact the GMI Counselling Unit.
